*Supervised by Prof. Hana Habib · February 2026 – Present*

- Investigated how ML practitioners understand and implement differential privacy, examining gaps between intended privacy guarantees and actual configurations using PyTorch and Opacus.
- Developed an experimental task where participants configure privacy parameters (epsilon, noise multiplier, clipping norm) and analyzed resulting privacy–utility tradeoffs, model accuracy, and implementation errors.
- Led IRB protocol preparation, study design documentation, recruitment planning, and the mixed-methods data collection and analysis plan for human subjects evaluation.
- Conducted thematic analysis, descriptive statistics, and cross-case synthesis of interview and observational data.

This work was accepted as a poster at SOUPS 2026 and the WiML Workshop @ NeurIPS 2026.
