*Google Research – University of Ibadan Joint HCI Capacity Building Program · 2024*

MedBuddy investigates how AI systems can support learning in resource-constrained medical education settings. I led the project, designing and running qualitative user research (interviews and observations) with medical students and faculty to understand the cognitive challenges of clinical learning, and analyzing the resulting data.

The findings were published in *ACM SIGACCESS Accessibility and Computing*, Issue 138: [Challenges and Opportunities in Designing Medical Learning Support Tools for Clinical Student Groups in the Global South](https://dl.acm.org/doi/abs/10.1145/3703599.3703601).
