- Led the MedBuddy project, an AI-driven mobile learning application investigating how AI systems support learning in resource-constrained medical education settings.
- Designed and executed qualitative user research with medical students and faculty, conducting interviews and observations to understand cognitive challenges in clinical learning.
- Conducted qualitative and quantitative data analysis on interview and observational data.
- Co-authored a peer-reviewed publication on AI-supported medical learning systems for clinical student groups in the Global South ([ACM SIGACCESS Accessibility and Computing, 2024](https://dl.acm.org/doi/abs/10.1145/3703599.3703601)).
